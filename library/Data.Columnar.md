# Data.Columnar

Columnar leaves over typed arrays, zipped into nested pairs that share their buffers. A length mismatch is None.

<div class="doc-mod-bar"><div class="doc-mod-bar-primary"><span class="support-tag support-tag--current">cinch/stdlib</span> <code>import Data.Columnar</code></div><div class="doc-mod-bar-links"><a href="/packages/stdlib/Data.Columnar/source/">View source</a><a href="/packages/search/?q=%2BData.Columnar">Search module</a><a href="/reference-sources/Data.Columnar.cinch" download="Data.Columnar.cinch">Download .cinch</a><a href="/packages/stdlib/">All stdlib modules</a></div></div>

**Dependencies:** [Data.Array](Data.Array.md), [Data.Core](Data.Core.md).

<details class="doc-notes"><summary>Module notes</summary>

A leaf is one typed array. Zip composes any number of heterogeneous
columns as nested pairs, sharing their buffers. Length mismatch is None.
