<details class="doc-notes"><summary>Module notes</summary>

Unicode 17.0.0 immutable classification and simple-case source data.
Generated by tooling/generate_unicode_tables.py. Provenance and original
licensing: tooling/data/unicode17-tables.json and unicode17-BSD-LICENSE.txt.
Ordinary bounded scalar pages lower to private read-only tables; no backing
pointer, mutable buffer or runtime full-table copy enters the public API.
