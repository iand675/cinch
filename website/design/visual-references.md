# Visual reference study

## Sources

- User-supplied military maintenance manual spread: numbered sections, figure captions, leader lines, crosshatching, dashed construction lines, and technical drawings with explicit part labels.
- [Silo — Extending Worlds: Silos 17 & 18, Apple TV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_tHrcff6BgI): downloaded the complete 23:54 visual stream at 720p to `/private/tmp/cinch-design-references/silo-reference.mp4`. This reference copy has no audio. Reviewed a 20-frame overview across the film and larger frames at 00:45, 04:55, 16:55, 17:55, 19:00, and 20:45.
- [make3 — Lumon Industries WoeMeter](https://make3.co/work/woemeter): inset instrument windows, close-fitting side caps, controlled dial markings, and continuous shaped housings.
- [Jake Parker — early commercial computers](https://www.mrjakeparker.com/blog/2024/3/4/the-cassette-futurism-design-aesthetic-of-early-commercial-computers): sculpted keyboard decks, recessed displays, and manufactured plastic curves.

Reference contact sheets are in `screenshots/references/`. They are study material and are not included in the public site assets.
