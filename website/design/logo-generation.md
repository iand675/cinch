# Cinch instrument wordmark

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from the original supplied Cinch logo. The selected version is opaque on pure black; the site uses screen blending on its dark headers and footer. This avoids the edge artifacts in the transparent trials. The original gold wordmark is retained.

## Initial flat-colour prompt

Create a clean production wordmark by restyling the supplied Cinch logo. Preserve its recognizable connected italic Cinch lettering and wide silhouette: sweeping capital C, looping ribbon swashes on both sides, tall h ascender. Exact text Cinch, no other text. The style is flat 1960s/70s scientific-instrument screen printing: warm ivory ribbon (#EEE6D5) with one narrow orange inlay (#E58B4E), precise clean geometric curves, readable counters, no bevels, no gradients, no shadows, no gold edging. Render on a completely uniform pure black (#000000) background, opaque black also inside every letter counter and loop. Absolutely no texture, distress, dust, speckles, fragments, halos or decorative elements outside the cleanly bounded logo. ONE horizontal logo filling the width of a wide canvas with modest margins; not a presentation sheet or mockup. Keep all edges crisp and the black negative space perfectly clean.
